# AI image prompts — homepage

Photo direction for the homepage's real-photography slots, to replace the generated placeholders
under `media/placeholders/` (see `ASSETS_NEEDED.md` for file formats, dimensions and size budgets —
this file only covers subject/lighting/composition direction and SEO alt text). Case study images
are shared with the case studies list/detail pages, so they're included here even though they only
partially show on the homepage.

Strict style for every prompt below: 100% photorealistic commercial photography, shot on a 35mm or
50mm lens, natural studio or on-site lighting, real UK tradespeople with authentic skin texture and
work-worn PPE/workwear — no 3D renders, no illustration, no anime, no cartoon style, no artificially
glossy/plastic skin. Do not render any manufacturer logo, wordmark or badge visible on equipment,
vans or clothing (brand identity is licensed separately, not to be invented by the image generator).
